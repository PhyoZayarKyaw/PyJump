WHAT IS IT?

PyJump is a 2D platformer that teaches foundational Python — data types, conditionals, and loops — through mechanics that directly embody each concept's logic, rather than isolated exercises bolted onto unrelated gameplay.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Built for science students with little to no prior programming experience — learners who already think in formulas and logic, but haven't yet met Python. No CS background required to press start.

TEACH STAGE → TEST STAGE
Each stage teaches one concept, then tests it immediately — before moving to the next
STEP 1TEACH STAGE
Introduces a new concept through low-stakes gameplay with clear instruction and explanation.
STEP 2TEST STAGE
Immediately checks whether the concept actually landed — same idea, no more instructions, with additional challenges.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

  • RQ1 How effective is a 2D platformer serious game in teaching fundamental computer science concepts?
  • RQ2 What learning gain do students achieve, as measured by pre-test and post-test performance?
  • RQ3 How does the game perform in the eyes of the students as a learning tool?
METHOD: pre-test → gameplay → post-test → feedback questionnaire
